🧹 Broom Race 🧹

Players
Left player
Right player
Move your body left and right to steer your broom 🧹 Touch the golden rings!
Ring = 1 point.   Big ring with a star = 3 points.
Both hands up high 🙌 = BOOST! Double points for 3 seconds.
Storm clouds and branches (Hard only) = minus 2. Fly around them!
Keys: Space = start, Esc = back to menu, F = full screen, M = music on/off, H = all games
Test keys: ← → = left broom, A D = right broom, B = boost
