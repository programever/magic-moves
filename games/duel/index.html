Welcome to the Dueling Club! Silly spells only: bubbles 🫧 feathers 🪶 frogs 🐸 tickles 😆
✨ Right hand = wand. Flick it fast toward the other side to cast a spell.
🛡️ Left hand up = shield. Raise it above your shoulder. Protego! Block = +1 point. Hit = +2 points.
1 player: duel the wobbly training scarecrow.
Keys: Space = start, Esc = menu, F = full screen, M = music, H = all games.
Test: mouse = left wand (click or flick = cast), Z = left cast, hold X = left shield, N = right cast, hold , (comma) = right shield
